About Us

Stadin Fixarit Oy is a reliable and efficient service provider in Helsinki and the capital region when you need help fast.

We are a trained and experienced team with strong expertise in both property maintenance and the construction industry. We complete our work smoothly, professionally, and without unnecessary delays.

Our goal is to make everyday life easier for our customers. We respond quickly to inquiries and can often be on-site on short notice. Whether it’s a small installation, yard work, or a repair job, we handle it efficiently and as agreed from start to finish.

We work carefully and cleanly, but above all practically – focusing on results that work and last over time. With us, you get smooth service, clear pricing, and a team that gets the job done properly.
