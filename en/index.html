Our Services

We offer reliable and easy-to-order services for homes, housing cooperatives, and businesses in Helsinki and throughout the Greater Helsinki area – quickly, flexibly, and as agreed.

Many of our services qualify for the Finnish household tax deduction (kotitalousvähennys).

Handyman Services

Smooth and precise installations for your everyday home needs – fast and reliably.

  • Mounting paintings, shelves, and mirrors
  • Assembling, moving, and dismantling furniture
  • Installation and setup of household appliances
  • Installing curtain rails, tracks, and light fixtures
  • Opening and cleaning drains
  • Other minor home installations

Yard Services

Care and maintenance of gardens and outdoor areas reliably across all seasons.

  • Yard cleaning and leaf raking
  • Lawn mowing and bush trimming
  • Tree felling and brush clearing
  • Washing roofs, facades, and outbuildings
  • Snow removal and salting/gritting

Renovation Services

Neat renovations completed on schedule with expert craftsmanship – from small repairs to complete space renovations.

  • Floor installation and surface renewals
  • Installing doors, moldings, and fixtures
  • Minor bathroom and kitchen renovations
  • Painting and surface refurbishments
  • Demolition and repair work

Gravestone Services

Dignified and meticulous gravestone care – carried out with respect and professional expertise.

  • Straightening and maintenance of gravestones
  • Cleaning and restoration of gravestones
  • Tidying up and upkeep of grave sites

Business Services

Flexible resource and maintenance services for businesses, construction companies, and housing cooperatives – as a one-time service or via ongoing contracts.
