Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

We provide home installation services, yard work, renovation services, grave maintenance services, as well as property and business services. We handle both small everyday tasks and larger projects reliably and as agreed.

What is included in home installation services?

Furniture assembly, TV mounting, shelves, pictures, lighting installation, appliance installation, and small home repairs.

What do yard services include?

Garden cleaning, lawn mowing, hedge trimming, snow removal, gritting, tree cutting, and clearing work.

What kind of renovation work do you do?

Flooring installation, painting, surface renovations, bathroom and kitchen repairs, as well as general refurbishment work.

What do grave maintenance services include?

Cleaning, straightening and restoring gravestones, as well as maintaining and tidying grave sites with respect and care.

Do you provide services for businesses?

Yes. We offer property maintenance, renovation work, yard services, and ongoing maintenance for companies and housing associations.

How quickly can I get service?

Often within the same day or the next day. For urgent cases, we aim to arrange service as quickly as possible.

How much do your services cost?

Prices usually start from 55 €/hour or are agreed as a fixed price depending on the work. Clear pricing with no hidden fees.

Are your services eligible for household tax credit?

Yes, most of our services are eligible for the household tax credit in Finland.

Is your work insured?

Yes. All our work is fully liability insured and carried out professionally and safely.
