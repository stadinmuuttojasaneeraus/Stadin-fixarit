Property Maintenance for Businesses

We provide comprehensive property maintenance services for businesses in Helsinki and the capital region. We ensure the functionality, cleanliness, and safety of your premises year-round, and respond quickly to urgent needs. We can work directly on assignment or act as additional support during busy periods.

Yard Maintenance and Outdoor Areas

The cleanliness and safety of outdoor areas require continuous attention. We maintain business outdoor areas regularly and handle urgent situations when extra resources are needed, for example during seasonal peaks or unexpected demand.

  • Yard cleaning and general maintenance
  • Snow removal and sanding
  • Lawn and planting maintenance
  • Outdoor area presentation and cleanliness

Renovation and Repair Work

We carry out renovation and repair work for businesses efficiently and flexibly. We handle both planned projects and urgent repairs, and can quickly provide workforce support when needed.

  • Office and facility surface renovations
  • Maintenance painting and repairs
  • Space modifications and upgrades
  • Urgent repair and emergency jobs

Flexible Partner and Additional Workforce

We act both as a long-term partner and for individual assignments. We can work directly under client orders or support your company during resource shortages and peak workload periods.
