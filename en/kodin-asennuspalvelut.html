Home Installation & Maintenance Services

Careful, insured, and reliable help for all your everyday home installations.

Furniture Assembly

We assemble furniture from IKEA, JYSK, and other brands carefully and correctly according to the instructions.

We set everything up completely ready for use – saving you from any stress or effort. If needed, we can also haul away packaging waste for recycling.

Wall & Ceiling Mountings

We securely install TVs, curtain rods, lamps, and heavy mirrors on all types of wall materials (drywall, concrete, brick, and wood).

We always use professional-grade anchors and wall plugs chosen for your specific wall material, ensuring that the installation holds the weight safely and cleanly without damage to your walls.

Drain Unclogging & Cleaning

We open and clean clogged drains in kitchens, bathrooms, and utility rooms quickly, effectively, and neatly without leaving a mess.

Beyond cleaning, we always verify the perfect tightness of all pipe connections to prevent water leaks and guarantee a smooth, continuous water flow.

Appliance Installation

We install washing machines and dishwashers safely and ready for use in accordance with manufacturer guidelines.

We take care of the connections and ensure the appliance is correctly leveled. All our work is fully covered by liability insurance, so you can enjoy everyday convenience with peace of mind.

Minor Maintenance & Quick Fixes

We help you with all the small problems and repairs around the house – like changing door handles, trim, or lightbulbs.

We offer flexible help for unexpected everyday needs at home. No job is too small – we handle all minor fixes in one go.

Our services cover a wide range of minor home installation and maintenance needs – basically all the small fixes that pop up in daily life for which you need fast and reliable help.
