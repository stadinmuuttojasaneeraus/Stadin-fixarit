About Us

Stadin Fixarit Oy provides practical and reliable services for homes, housing associations, and businesses in Helsinki and the capital region.

We combine construction expertise, property maintenance experience, and excellent customer service. From small everyday tasks to larger renovation projects, we handle every job professionally and with clear communication.

Our customers especially appreciate our quick response times, tidy workmanship, and the fact that we stick to agreed schedules and pricing.

We work responsibly, practically, and always with the customer’s needs in mind – our goal is smooth service and results you can genuinely rely on.

Why Choose Us

Communication

We serve our customers fluently in Finnish, Swedish, and English when needed. We respond quickly to enquiries and keep communication clear throughout the entire process – from the first contact to the finished result.

Professional Expertise

We have strong hands-on experience in property maintenance and the construction industry. We know how to complete jobs correctly, safely, and efficiently – without unnecessary middlemen or guesswork. Every project is carried out carefully and with professional pride.

Quality Guarantee

We focus on delivering clean, durable, and high-quality results in every project. We never leave work half-finished and always ensure the final outcome is functional, carefully completed, and approved by the customer. Our work is fully insured and responsibly delivered.

Reliability & Speed

We act quickly when help is needed – often even at short notice. We stick to agreed schedules and pricing, with no unpleasant surprises. Our priority is making sure customers get the help they need, when they need it most.
