About Us

Stadin Fixarit Oy is a reliable and efficient service provider in Helsinki and the entire capital region when you need help quickly and without hassle.

We are a trained and experienced team with strong expertise in both property maintenance and construction work. We carry out every job smoothly, professionally, and without unnecessary delays.

Our goal is simple – to make everyday life easier for our customers. We respond quickly to inquiries and can often be on-site the same or next day. Whether it’s a small installation, yard work, or repair, we handle the job efficiently from start to finish.

We work carefully and neatly, but above all practically – focusing on results that are not only visually good but also durable and functional. With us, you get clear pricing, smooth communication, and a partner who actually gets the job done.
