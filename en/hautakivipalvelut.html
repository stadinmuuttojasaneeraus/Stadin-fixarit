Gravestone Services in Helsinki

Respectful and careful maintenance of gravestones and burial sites. We help keep the memory of your loved ones clean, dignified, and well cared for throughout the year.

Request a Quote

Gravestone straightening and foundation work

Has a gravestone tilted or sunk over time? We carefully straighten gravestones and perform necessary foundation work to ensure the stone remains stable and dignified for many years to come.

Gravestone cleaning and maintenance

Weather and time leave their marks on stone surfaces. We gently and professionally clean gravestones, restoring their dignified and neat appearance without damaging the structure.

Year-round grave site care

We maintain the cleanliness and condition of grave sites according to the season. We remove leaves, clean the area, and ensure the resting place remains respectful and peaceful.

Ongoing grave maintenance service

Want to ensure the grave site is always well maintained? We offer continuous maintenance services, including weed removal, cleaning, and flower planting. This ensures a well-kept and peaceful memorial site at all times.
