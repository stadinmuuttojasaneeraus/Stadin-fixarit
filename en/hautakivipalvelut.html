Gravestone services and grave site maintenance in the metropolitan area

Dignified, respectful, and meticulous dedication to your loved one’s final resting place.

Gravestone cleaning and maintenance

The Nordic climate, air pollution, and the passage of time inevitably leave their mark on the stone's surface. We clean gravestones with extreme care, sensitivity, and professional expertise.

We remove moss, lichen, and stubborn dirt, restoring the memorial's dignified, beautiful, and pristine appearance without damaging the stone’s delicate surface structure or engravings.

Gravestone straightening and foundation work

Has the memorial tilted, shifted, or sunk due to soil movement and time? We perform gravestone straightening and alignment with the utmost care, safety, and precision.

We carry out the necessary ground and foundation work, straightening the stone so it remains stable and stands with dignity long into the future.

Grave site maintenance and tidying

We look after the general cleanliness, condition, and dignity of the grave site, always with sensitivity and tailored to the seasons.

We remove fallen leaves, pine branches, withered flowers, and old candles, ensuring the memorial site remains a peaceful, beautiful, and respectful environment for relatives to visit.

Ongoing grave site care services

Would you like to ensure that your loved one's memorial site remains beautiful and well-tended even when you are unable to visit in person?

We offer reliable and regular care services, including weeding, soil replacement, tidying, as well as planting and watering flowers. This allows you to always arrive at a peaceful and beautifully maintained memorial.

We provide sensitive and professional gravestone services in Helsinki and the entire metropolitan area—ranging from individual cleanings and straightens to ongoing seasonal care contracts.

We professionally clean and wash gravestones to remove moss and lichen, straighten and support tilted or sunken stones, and restore engravings and gilding.

We operate flexibly in all cemeteries in Helsinki, Espoo, Vantaa, Kauniainen, and across the entire metropolitan area as agreed.

According to the rules of the Tax Administration, work carried out in cemeteries is unfortunately not eligible for a household tax deduction, as the work is not performed in your own home or yard. Therefore, we always strive to keep our pricing as clear and affordable as possible.

Cleaning and straightening of headstones are done from spring to autumn when the ground is unfrozen. Painting and gilding of text require completely dry and warm weather to ensure a durable and beautiful result.

Simple cleaning and straightening can be done at an hourly rate (starting from €55/h incl. VAT). If the stone requires multiple steps, such as washing, straightening, and regilding, we will calculate a clear, fixed package offer for you.
