Yard & Gardening Services

Careful, insured, and reliable professional help for all your yard and garden needs year-round.

Lawn and Plant Care Services

Comprehensive and regular maintenance ensures that lawns, bushes, and trees remain vibrant, healthy, and pristine throughout the growing season.

We tailor maintenance procedures to the needs of your yard's vegetation. Our service includes professional lawn mowing, fertilizing, liming, aeration, and plant disease prevention. Through long-term care, we guarantee a lush, green lawn and a thriving garden that increases the value of the entire property.

Tree and Bush Felling, Pruning, and Removal

Professional tree felling, maintenance pruning, and hedge cutting back are always performed safely, including the removal of all garden waste.

Whether it's felling a dangerous tree casting too much shadow, pruning damaged branches, or shaping an overgrown hedge, we carry out the work with modern and safe equipment. We always ensure complete safety and clean up thoroughly, transporting all branches, trunks, and cuttings directly to proper recycling for you.

Tree and Bush Planting Works

We plan and execute the planting of new trees, ornamental shrubs, and hedge plants correctly to guarantee the best growing conditions.

Correct planting methods and high-quality soil are crucial for plants to thrive. We take care of soil improvement, correct planting depth, fertilization, and initial watering for you. We help you choose the plant species best suited to the conditions of your plot, bringing long-lasting beauty, privacy, and structure to your yard.

Ecological Washing of Terraces, Facades, and Roofs

We clean your property's exterior surfaces effectively and gently using eco-friendly, biodegradable, and surface-protecting detergents.

We remove dirt, moss, algae, and other weather-induced growth from terraces, facade materials, and roof surfaces. Our ecological methods deep-clean surfaces and leave long-lasting protection against new growth, without wearing down materials or harming your yard's delicate plantings or soil.

Winter Maintenance of Pathways and Roofs

Prevent damage and ensure safety by taking care of snow removal on driveways, de-icing/gritting, and snow loads on roofs.

We look after your property's safety even in winter's challenging conditions. Our services include efficient snow plowing of driveways and pathways, manual snow shoveling from your doorstep, regular sanding/salting to prevent slipping, and controlled, supervised snow removal from roofs before it poses a threat to structures or passersby.

Versatile Yard Work and General Property Tidiness

We offer flexible help with all seasonal yard chores, such as leaf raking, trash pickup, and tidying the general appearance of outdoor areas.

We ensure your plot looks presentable year-round. We perform efficient spring and autumn leaf raking, clearing of fallen branches, sweeping of brickwork and tiles, and removal of any loose litter. No maintenance task is too small for us – we make sure your outdoor areas always remain clean, safe, and welcoming.

Our comprehensive property and gardening services adapt flexibly to your needs in all seasons. We offer reliable, professional, and insured assistance for all yard maintenance and renovation work.
